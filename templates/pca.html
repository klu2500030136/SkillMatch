Understanding PCA

What is PCA?

PCA is a technique that reduces many related features into a smaller number of new components while trying to keep most of the information. Think of it like compressing a large collection of information into fewer summaries.

Why do we scale the data?

PCA looks at variation in the data. If one feature uses much larger numbers than another, it can dominate the calculation. Scaling puts features on a comparable scale before PCA.

What does "explained variance" mean?

It tells us how much of the information/variation in the original data is represented by each principal component. A higher percentage means that component captures more of the variation.

What are loadings?

Loadings show how strongly each original processed feature contributes to a principal component. A larger absolute loading means a stronger contribution.
