What is 5-Fold Cross Validation?

Instead of training and checking a model only once, we divide the training data into five parts. The model is trained five times. Each time, one part is used for validation and the other four parts are used for training.

Fold 1
Train: F2-F5
Val: F1
Fold 2
Train: F1,F3-F5
Val: F2
Fold 3
Train: F1-F2,F4-F5
Val: F3
Fold 4
Train: F1-F3,F5
Val: F4
Fold 5
Train: F1-F4
Val: F5

Every training record gets a chance to be part of the validation set. This gives a more reliable estimate of model performance.

In Simple Words

We split the training data into five parts. The model learns from four parts and is checked using the remaining part. We repeat this five times so every part gets a turn for validation. Finally, we calculate the average and variation of the results.

Mean

Shows the average performance across the five folds.

Standard Deviation

Shows how much the model's performance changed from one fold to another.
