What Do These Numbers Mean?

Accuracy

How often was the model correct overall across all predictions?

Precision

When the model predicted Attrition, how often was it actually correct?

Recall

How many of the employees who actually had Attrition did the model correctly identify?

F1-Score

A combined measure that balances precision and recall into a single score.

Confusion Matrix

Shows exactly which predictions were correct and which were mistakes (TN, FP, FN, TP).

ROC-AUC

Measures how well the model separates the two classes across different decision thresholds. Higher is better.
